Ricardo [Apellido]

PhD Researcher, Applied Economics — Universidad de Oviedo

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Applied Economics at the Universidad de Oviedo, based in Asturias, Spain. My dissertation, Three Essays on Left-Behind Places, studies how declining and peripheral regions adjust — through housing markets, political behaviour, and the labour market — to economic and demographic shocks.

My work combines panel econometrics (system-GMM, instrumental variables, event-study and shift-share designs) with large administrative and geospatial datasets, using R and Python. Across three strands of research I study local housing markets under immigration pressure, the political economy of populism in declining regions, and how the green and digital transition reshapes regional labour markets.
